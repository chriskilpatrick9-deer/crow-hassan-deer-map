Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 6 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 15.6 deer per 100 hours and recorded 16 harvests in 466 sits. With only 6 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive.
  • The colder days (Nov 13–15, lows 19–25°F) had 23.0 per 100 hours and 10 harvests in 221 sits (4.5 per 100 sits), compared with 7.0 per 100 hours and 6 harvests in 245 sits (2.4 per 100 sits) on the warmer days (Oct 9–11, lows 44–67°F). The two sets fell in different hunt periods and also differed in rising versus falling pressure and calmer versus windier conditions, so these factors cannot be separated from temperature.
  • Sits that started in the morning had 14.7 per 100 hours and 7 harvests in 282 sits (2.5 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 47°F. Afternoon sits had 17.9 per 100 hours and 9 harvests in 184 sits (4.9 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 62°F.
  • The highest sighting rate was on Nov 13 (27.5 per 100 hours, 4 harvests, 19–39°F, NNE at 3.8 mph, rising pressure).
