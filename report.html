2026 Crow-Hassan Deer Hunt: Final Coordinator Report

Sixteen harvest entries over six hunt days at Crow-Hassan Park Reserve (MBRB hunt 10). Hunters logged 371 deer seen in 2,364 hours, or 15.7 per 100 hours. Fifteen of the 16 entries are listed as recovered; one Nov 15 Zone 3 adult buck is listed as wounded.

Findings

  • The 2nd period was much busier than the 1st. November sits saw 23.0 deer per 100 hours, more than three times the October rate of 6.7, and 10 of the 16 harvests came then. October was also unusually warm, with highs in the 70s and 90s.
  • Afternoon sits had a modest edge: 17.9 seen per 100 hours when the sit started at noon or later, versus 14.8 in the morning. That split is by sit start, not the time each deer was seen. Zone 7 afternoons were the strongest (31.2). Zone 2 was better in the morning (19.7 versus 7.5). Zone 4’s afternoon rate (2.8) rests on only 35 hours.
  • In the 2nd period, north, northeast, and northwest winds were best, at about 27 to 28 seen per 100 hours (241, 170, and 100 hours of sits). South wind was the slowest large sample, at 17.7 (358 hours). Southeast was in between, at 22.7. East, west, and southwest had little or no 2nd-period time and should not be used. October was slow in every direction with a decent sample (about 6 to 10 per 100 hours), and most of that period blew from the south or southeast, so season and wind are mixed in the all-season totals.
